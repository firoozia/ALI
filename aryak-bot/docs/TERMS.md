# Atossa ARYAK: Terms of Service

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Atossa ARYAK is the internal publishing tool of ARYAK (https://aryak.ae), a supplier of decorative films and materials in the
United Arab Emirates. It publishes ARYAK's own design pictures and videos to ARYAK's own social media accounts, including its
TikTok account, and only after the ARYAK owner has approved each post.

1. The tool is used only by ARYAK and only with accounts that belong to ARYAK. It is not offered to other people or companies.
2. Every video it publishes is ARYAK's own content (design ideas made for ARYAK, with music made for ARYAK). Design pictures
   made with AI are described as design ideas in their captions.
3. Nothing is published without the explicit approval of the ARYAK owner.
4. The tool follows the TikTok Terms of Service, the TikTok Community Guidelines and the TikTok Developer Terms of Service.
5. ARYAK may change or stop the tool at any time.
