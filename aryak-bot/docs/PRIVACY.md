# Atossa ARYAK: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Atossa ARYAK is the internal publishing tool of ARYAK, a supplier of decorative films and materials to furniture and carpentry
workshops in the United Arab Emirates (https://aryak.ae). It is used only by ARYAK to publish ARYAK's own design pictures and
videos to ARYAK's own social media accounts, including its TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

## What we collect

When the ARYAK TikTok account is connected, TikTok gives the tool an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID.
These are used only to upload and publish videos that the ARYAK owner has approved, and to read whether an upload succeeded.
We do not collect, read or store any information about other TikTok users, their videos, comments or messages.

## How it is stored

The tokens are kept on ARYAK's own server, readable only by the tool, and are never shared with anyone else.
They are deleted when the account is disconnected or when ARYAK stops using the tool.

## Your choices

The connected account can be disconnected at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions).
