# Parametric Cabinet-Design Software (Carcass/Unit Generators) — Free & Freemium Catalog

Scope note: Focus is on tools that let a user define a cabinet UNIT by parameters (W×H×D, door/drawer/shelf counts, construction method) and auto-generate parts + a cut-list. "Free" is distinguished from "trial" and "freemium" throughout. Information current as of 2024–2026 where sourced; several vendor terms could only be partially verified because direct page fetches (WebFetch) were unavailable this session — those are flagged. Primary-site pricing should be re-verified for any purchase decision.

## Which dedicated cabinet programs have free versions/trials, and what do they generate?

### Takeaway
Of the dedicated cabinet CAD packages, only **PolyBoard** has a genuine free tier (full design, manufacturing output locked), and **Thermwood eCabinet Systems** is genuinely free (full, after registration). The rest — Mozaik, SketchList 3D, CabinetSense, Cabinet Pro, KCDw, Microvellum — are paid/subscription with trials only (or no trial, in Microvellum's case). All of these model a unit→parts and produce cut-lists; they differ mainly in CNC/DXF output and construction-method flexibility.

### Cited Findings

**PolyBoard (Wood Designer / Boole & Partners) — FREE tier (freemium), Windows**
- Free version enables "100% of the design features," but manufacturing output (cut list, DXF of parts) is locked behind the paid license; free-version designs can later be opened in the paid software — [wooddesigner.org via WebSearch](https://wooddesigner.org/cabinet-cutlist-software/)
- Vendor's paid CabinetFile service will process a free-version design and return a complete PDF cutting list, dimensioned PDF drawings, and DXF files of all separately cut parts (tied to "Premium Support" — may be a separate charge) — [wooddesigner.org/cabinetfile-service](https://wooddesigner.org/cabinetfile-service/)
- Parametric behavior: resizing a cabinet automatically updates all internal components (shelves, doors, drawers update together with the carcass) — [wooddesigner.org](https://wooddesigner.org/cabinet-cutlist-software/)
- Companion product OptiCut converts the cut list into sequential cutting maps to minimize waste — [wooddesigner.org](https://wooddesigner.org/cabinet-cutlist-software/)

**Thermwood eCabinet Systems — genuinely FREE, Windows**
- Free to use after registering online at ecabinetsystems.com (per 2016 Wood Shop News; terms old, re-verify) — [Wood Shop News 2016 via WebSearch](https://lsc-pagepro.mydigitalpublication.com/article/The+Cutting+Edge+/2588866/339749/article.html)
- Features: 3D design, detailed shop drawings and cut lists, CNC toolpaths — [forestsource.io/software/thermwood-ecabinet-systems](https://forestsource.io/software/thermwood-ecabinet-systems)
- Usable without a CNC (conventional saws / "saw shops"); configured for Thermwood CNC but also compatible with ShopBot machines — [Fine Woodworking forum via WebSearch](https://www.finewoodworking.com/forum/cabinet-software)
- Caveats (forum): resource-heavy ("need a monster computer"), steep learning curve — [sawmillcreek.org via WebSearch](https://sawmillcreek.org/threads/good-software.77809/)

**Mozaik (now Cyncly) — PAID subscription, NO free trial (paid 3-month trial), Windows**
- No free version; starts with a 3-month PAID trial that converts to a 1-year subscription unless cancelled — [mozaiksoftware.com via WebSearch](https://www.mozaiksoftware.com/products/mozaik-manufacturing); [cyncly.com/product-overviews/mozaik](https://www.cyncly.com/en/product-overviews/mozaik/)
- Pricing (tiered, USD/month): Mozaik Manufacturing ~$125/mo (non-CNC / table-saw shops); Mozaik CNC ~$225/mo; plus Mozaik Enterprise — [mozaiksoftware.com via WebSearch](https://www.mozaiksoftware.com/products/mozaik-manufacturing)
- Non-CNC tier generates door lists, drawer box lists, cut lists, hardware requirements, material requirements, submittal drawings — [cyncly.com/en/mozaik/products/mozaik-manufacturing](https://cyncly.com/en/mozaik/products/mozaik-manufacturing)
- Warning: "free download"/"MZ Free" and "cracked" pages in search are low-quality SEO/pirated content, not an official free edition — [Cyncly official via WebSearch](https://www.cyncly.com/en/product-overviews/mozaik/)

**SketchList 3D — PAID (one-time license tiers), free trial available, Windows + Mac**
- Free TRIAL available; no free version (per FitGap). Pricing conflicting across aggregators: Capterra lists a one-time flat rate from ~$250 with trial — Hobby ~$200, Pro ~$850; FitGap lists from $37.50/month — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/204375/SketchList-3D/); [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/054499/sketchlist-3d)
- Auto-generates layouts, spreadsheet, drawings, cut lists, reports; exports PDF or CSV; needs separate app for G-code — [Woodworkers Journal via WebSearch](https://www.woodworkersjournal.com/?p=19202)
- Cabinet-specific parametrics via the "Cabinet Wizard" add-on: adjust 70+ cabinet parameters including dimensions, joinery, door styles, materials — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/204375/SketchList-3D/)

**CabinetSense — PAID subscription (SketchUp plugin), 14-day trial, Windows + Mac**
- 14-day free trial (per older SketchUp forum post; re-verify at cabinetsensesoftware.com) — [SketchUp/sawmillcreek forum via WebSearch](https://sawmillcreek.org/threads/cabinet-sense-for-sketchup.307374/)
- Runs inside SketchUp Desktop (SketchUp 2021–2026); requires SketchUp Pro desktop (free SketchUp is browser-only and won't run extensions) — [myarchitectai 2026 comparison via WebSearch](https://www.myarchitectai.com/blog/cabinet-design-software)
- Automates production drawings, cut lists, sheet cutting diagrams, auto-labels all parts; exports DXF for CAM/CNC but NO toolpaths (needs separate CAM) — [forestsource.io/software/cabinetsense](https://forestsource.io/software/cabinetsense); [softwaresuggest.com/cabinetsense](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/cabinetsense)

**Cabinet Pro (Cabinet Pro LLC) — PAID, trials reported (terms old), Windows**
- Reported 30-day free trial that includes cut lists for both face-frame AND frameless construction, plus CNC code generation for any router (undated article; re-verify at cabinetpro.com) — [Woodshop News via WebSearch](https://www.woodshopnews.com/news/cabinet-pro-offers-free-trial)
- Separate older "5-day test drive" one-time offer also documented — [Woodshop News via WebSearch](https://woodshopnews.com/news/five-day-trial-available-from-cabinet-pro)
- Generates detailed cut lists, material/door reports, panel optimization, labels, and its OWN CNC code without a separate CAM interpreter — [forestsource.io/software/cabinet-pro](https://forestsource.io/software/cabinet-pro)

**KCDw — PAID, paid trial reported, Windows**
- Forum report: a trial cost ~$45 USD, "fully operational except could not print" (undated, likely outdated); purchase reportedly ~$5000, or ~$1000 down / $100/mo rent (old forum) — [Fine Woodworking forum via WebSearch](https://finehomebuilding.com/forum/cabinet-design-software)
- Not listed in the 2026 roundup; no official current pricing found — gap.

**Microvellum (Toolbox, runs inside AutoCAD) — PAID, quote-based, NO free trial found, Windows**
- Quote-based annual subscription (billed annually or monthly); sold as one complete package, no tiers; acquired by INNERGY Feb 2025 — [myarchitectai 2026 comparison via WebSearch](https://www.myarchitectai.com/blog/cabinet-design-software)
- No free trial mentioned in any source — gap.

### Inferences
- For a free-cost unit→parts/cut-list workflow among dedicated packages, only **PolyBoard (design free, cut-list paid/via service)** and **eCabinet Systems (fully free)** qualify; everything else is trial-gated.
- Cabinet Pro and eCabinet Systems are the only dedicated tools confirmed to emit CNC G-code themselves; CabinetSense/SketchList stop at DXF/CSV and need external CAM — relevant if the inspiration target only needs a cut-list, not toolpaths.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch PolyBoard's own page directly (WebFetch DNS failures), so exact free-tier limits and current CabinetFile pricing are from search snippets only.
- No current official KCDw or Microvellum pricing/trial pages found.

## Hardware-vendor FREE planners/configurators (Blum, Hettich, Häfele, GRASS) — do any output cut-lists or cabinet parts?

### Takeaway
Yes — the major European fittings vendors offer **free, browser-based** planners that output wooden-parts/cut lists, not just hardware layouts. **Hettich Plan** (free, no install, reportedly no registration) outputs a wooden parts list in PDF/XLS + fittings list + CAD drawings. **Blum's Cabinet Configurator** (successor to Dynaplan, free) outputs parts/cutting lists, production drawings, CAD/CAM and BXF files including cutting dimensions and drilling positions. Häfele and GRASS equivalents could not be confirmed as cut-list generators from available sources.

### Cited Findings

**Hettich Plan — FREE, browser-based**
- Browser-based configurator covering planning→production; usable without additional installation and (per one Hettich page) without registration — [hettich.com hettich-plan via WebSearch](https://web.hettich.com/en-dk/hettich-plan/about-hettich-plan); [hettich.com/en-au/services/hettich-plan](https://www.hettich.com/en-au/services/hettich-plan)
- Outputs: wooden parts list in PDF and XLS, a fittings order list, and CAD drawings of the carcase and components — [surfaceandpanel.com](https://www.surfaceandpanel.com/new-tool-for-digital-furniture-planning-hettich-plan/)
- Caveat: viewing/using the exported CAD drawings may require additional programs; listed version 1.1, with dated system requirements (Win XP–10) — [hettich.com via WebSearch](https://web.hettich.com/en-de/press/detail/hettich-helpful-e-tools-for-the-cabinet-maker)

**Blum Cabinet Configurator (successor to DYNAPLAN) — FREE, browser-based**
- Explicitly described as successor to "our tried and tested DYNAPLAN planning software" — [blum.com/nz Dynaplan page via WebSearch](https://www.blum.com/nz/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/dynaplan/)
- Produces parts and cutting lists, production drawings, CAD/CAM and BXF files, in digital or printed form — [blum.com NZ via WebSearch](https://www.blum.com/nz/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/dynaplan/)
- BXF export carries not just fitting info but manufacturing info for wooden parts: cutting dimensions and drilling positions; BXF is compatible with a growing number of partner software products — [blum.com HK/NZ via WebSearch](https://blum.com/hk/en/services/planning-construction-product-selection/dynaplan)
- Historical conflict: an older Thermwood forum post reported Blum had discontinued planning software ("hardware changes too rapidly"); that predates the revived Cabinet Configurator — [thermwood.com forum via WebSearch](https://www.thermwood.com/forums/viewtopic.php?p=66644)

**Häfele — not confirmed**
- No Häfele cut-list/parts planner surfaced in searches — gap.

**GRASS — not confirmed**
- No GRASS (or Nothegger) furniture planner / cut-list configurator found; search returned only unrelated tools (Cutlistor, CutList Optimizer) — [WebSearch returned no GRASS match](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1108711/Cutlistor)

### Inferences
- The free vendor configurators (Blum, Hettich) are the strongest free design-inspiration precedent for a "unit→parts + cut-list" generator that runs in a browser with no install — exactly the carcass-generator pattern, with manufacturing data (cut dimensions, drilling) exported via structured formats (XLS, BXF).
- BXF (Blum) and the PDF/XLS parts list (Hettich) show the output expectation: a per-part list with dimensions + drilling/hardware positions, not just a 3D render.

### Gaps
- Exact fields/layout of Blum's and Hettich's cut-list output not documented in sources.
- Häfele and GRASS cut-list capability unverified; likely they offer planners but confirmation not found.

## Free online kitchen/cabinet planners (IKEA, 2020, Prodboard, etc.) — parts/cut-list or layout only?

### Takeaway
The mainstream consumer planners (IKEA Home Planner, 2020) produce **layouts + an item/price list of orderable products**, NOT a shop cut-list of panels — and IKEA's item list even omits some fillers/side panels/hardware. A newer wave of free browser/mobile tools (Flatma, "Kitchen Maker", Blueprint) DOES generate true panel cut-lists. Prodboard and 2020's cut-list capability could not be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **IKEA Home Planner**: free, browser-based, no download; gives a 3D layout + 2D view and a parts/item list to ease ordering — but the list is of IKEA products/prices, not shop panels; it does NOT auto-add cover panels for sides/fillers and removing doors auto-removes hinges from the list — [plum-living.com ikea user guide via WebSearch](https://plum-living.com/uk/media/practice/ikea-user-guide); [somuchbetterwithage.com](https://somuchbetterwithage.com/ikea-kitchen-cabinets/)
- **Flatma** (free online kitchen planner): produces pricing, cut lists, drawings and production files directly from the design; DIY kitchen page lists cut-list generation and edge-banding layouts with PDF download — [flatma.com/en/kitchen](https://flatma.com/en/kitchen/); [flatma.com kitchen-planner article](https://flatma.com/es/articles/kitchen-planner/)
- **Kitchen Maker – Cut List & Panel Cutting** (browser): generates cut lists, optimizes panel cutting to reduce waste, previews assembly in interactive 3D — [producthunt.com](https://www.producthunt.com/products/kitchen-maker-cut-list-panel-cutting)
- **Blueprint: Cabinet Design** (iOS app): cut-list PDF export with every panel's final dimensions, material, grain direction, edge banding; DXF nesting export is a separate PAID add-on — [apps.apple.com/app/id6760829779](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760829779)
- **CutList Optimizer** (mobile/web): panel optimizer (furniture panels, steel, glass, fabric) exporting PDF, Excel, DXF — note this is an optimizer, takes a parts list as input, not a cabinet unit generator — [apps.apple.com/app/id6465744401](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6465744401)

### Inferences
- Consumer "kitchen planners" = layout + orderable-product list; they are NOT a design-inspiration source for a unit→parts generator because they never decompose a carcass into cut panels.
- The relevant inspiration tier is the newer browser tools (Flatma, Kitchen Maker) that explicitly take a parametric cabinet and emit a panel cut-list + edge-banding + optimization — the same target capability.

### Gaps
- Prodboard: no results found for its cut-list capability — gap.
- 2020 Design (consumer/pro): not surfaced in this session; typically a layout/design-and-quote tool, but cut-list capability unconfirmed here — gap.

## How do the better tools define a parametric cabinet "library/template" and construction method?

### Takeaway
The leading tools model construction as a set of selectable parameters on a cabinet template: a Box Construction choice (frameless/Euro vs face-frame), a Door/Drawer Overlay mode (full overlay with reveal, inset), and reveal/gap values that drive door/drawer-front size formulas. Mozaik and Chief Architect expose this cleanly; back-panel rebate is a known weak spot where naive parametric methods leave gaps.

### Cited Findings
- **Chief Architect**: cabinet dialog lets you pick Frameless under "Box Construction," then Full Overlay under "Door/Drawer Overlay" with a specified reveal value — [chiefarchitect.com KB-01365 frameless euro cabinets](https://www.chiefarchitect.com/support/article/KB-01365/creating-frameless-euro-style-cabinets.pdf)
- **Mozaik face parameters** (construction as separate parameters):
  - Frame Inset: doors/drawer fronts flush with the frame, sized from the inset amount
  - Frameless Inset: same but referenced against the case front
  - Full Overlay: door/drawer fronts calculated from the reveal parameters — [mozaik.support.cyncly.com face parameters via WebSearch](https://mozaik.support.cyncly.com/hc/en-us/articles/45270578248721)
- **Definitions (Blum)**: Overlay = how much bigger the door is than the cabinet frame; Reveal = the portion of cabinet frame left exposed after the door is attached — [Blum material via WebSearch](https://mozaik.support.cyncly.com/hc/en-us/articles/45270578248721)
- **Back-panel rebate is a known problem**: a Wood Designer/PolyBoard forum user reports a rebate in the back face can't be produced with the standard method; using inner tooling leaves a gap in the side panel; applying face frames from the library pushed the back panel away from the frame — [wooddesigner.org forum via WebSearch](https://wooddesigner.org/forum/polyboard/doors-side-panel/)
- **Face-frame attachment (Microvellum training)**: a face frame sits on the box defined by top/bottom/side overlays and attaches via dados or hardware arrays (content tied to a paid expansion module) — [microvellum.com face-frame overlays video via WebSearch](https://microvellum.com/resources/videos/mvu-elearning/face-frame-cabinet-overlays-and-construction-methods)

### Inferences
- The canonical parametric model for a cabinet unit = (1) a Box Construction type, (2) an Overlay/Inset mode, (3) reveal/gap values, and (4) joinery/back-panel method; door and drawer-front dimensions are derived formulas from box size + reveal/overlay, exactly the formula-driven approach a unit→parts generator should replicate.
- Back-panel rebate (groove vs full-back rebate) and the interaction between face-frame overlay and back-panel placement are the hard edge cases where existing tools stumble — worth explicit handling in any new generator.

### Gaps
- No single source gives a complete worked template combining rebate + overlay + reveal formulas; the Mozaik/Microvellum docs and Wood Designer forum threads are the closest, and the Mozaik page could not be fetched directly this session.

## Windows usability & design-inspiration relevance (summary)

### Takeaway
All dedicated packages here run on Windows (SketchList/CabinetSense also Mac); the free vendor configurators (Blum, Hettich) and newer browser tools are platform-agnostic. The most relevant inspirations for a cabinet unit→parts generator are the free, parameter-driven, cut-list-emitting tools.

### Cited Findings / Inferences
- Windows-native dedicated tools: PolyBoard, eCabinet Systems, Mozaik, Cabinet Pro, KCDw, Microvellum (AutoCAD-hosted), SketchList (also Mac), CabinetSense (SketchUp, Win+Mac) — per vendor/aggregator listings above.
- Browser/no-install (Windows via browser): Hettich Plan, Blum Cabinet Configurator, Flatma, Kitchen Maker, CutList Optimizer.
- Strongest design-inspiration set for a free unit→parts/cut-list generator: **Blum Cabinet Configurator** and **Hettich Plan** (free, parametric unit→parts→cut-list+drilling, structured export), **PolyBoard** (true parametric carcass model, free design tier), **eCabinet Systems** (fully free, full pipeline incl. cut-lists), and the parameter model from **Mozaik/Chief Architect** (clean Box Construction + Overlay + reveal formula exposition).

### Gaps
- Direct vendor-page verification (WebFetch) was unavailable this session due to DNS/proxy errors; trial lengths and some pricing come from aggregators/undated articles and should be re-verified on official sites before relying on them.
